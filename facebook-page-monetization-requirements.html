Facebook Page Monetization Requirements Explained

By GameTipsHub Team | Updated October 2026

If you want to earn from a Facebook Page, the first step is understanding the requirements. Meta changes its monetization rules from time to time, so this guide focuses on the general checks you should make rather than fixed numbers that may be out of date. Always confirm the current requirements inside your own Page or professional dashboard before you make plans.

Check your eligibility in your dashboard

Meta shows monetization status inside your Page or Creator Studio. Open the monetization section and read the current list of requirements. Eligibility can depend on your location, follower count, content type and account age, so check your own dashboard instead of relying on old blog posts.

Follower and engagement requirements

Many monetization programs look at follower numbers, engagement and recent activity. A Page with active followers who watch and react usually performs better than a Page with many inactive likes. Focus on quality content that gets real interaction.

Original content requirements

Meta reviews content for originality. Reposting other creators' videos without adding value can block monetization. Make your own videos, photos and captions, and credit sources properly when you use outside material.

Community standards and page health

Pages that repeatedly break community standards can lose access to monetization tools. Avoid misleading claims, hate speech, spam and copyrighted material you do not have rights to. Keep your Page clean and your posting honest.

Account security and admin access

Use strong passwords, turn on two-factor authentication and only give admin roles to people you trust. Never share your login details with anyone who offers to help you get approved faster. Real help comes through official Meta support channels.

What to do if you are not eligible yet

If the dashboard says you are not eligible, focus on what it asks for: consistent original posts, growing followers and better engagement. Keep posting for a few months and check again. There is no shortcut that guarantees approval.

Watch for fake approval services

Some sites and people claim they can get your Page monetized for a fee. These offers are usually scams and can put your account at risk. Never pay for approval, and do not trust anyone who asks for your password or admin access in exchange for a promise.

Plan income streams around eligibility

Do not depend on one monetization feature alone. Use affiliate links, brand deals or services while you grow toward eligibility. A diversified plan gives you income even if a program changes its rules.

Keep records and read policy updates

Save screenshots of your monetization status and any payment notices. Subscribe to official updates from Meta's business and creator resources. If your earnings change or a payment is missing, use the official support tools in your dashboard.

A realistic timeline

Many Pages take months to build a real audience and meet requirements. Set a monthly check-in, post steadily and track your growth. The strongest Pages are built on helpful content that people want to see again, not on tricks.

Build a posting routine you can keep

Monetization reviews reward Pages that stay active over time, not Pages that post ten times in one week and then disappear. Pick a schedule you can keep, such as three useful posts a week, and plan topics ahead so you are not rushing. A steady routine also gives you clear data to check each month, so you can see what your audience responds to and do more of it.
